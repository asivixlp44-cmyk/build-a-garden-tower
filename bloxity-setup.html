Description
Get your own glass greenhouse and grow the tallest garden of the server! Buy seeds at the Seed Shop in the market, plant them, wait for the fruit to ripen and step on your plants to harvest them. Your harvest piles up in the FRUITS crates: carry them to the market and sell them for Cash. Buy better seeds (23 of them, from the Common Apple to the Mythic Starfruit Tree), upgrade every floor and build new floors on top, up to the Sky Garden on Floor 8. Roll Golden, Frozen, Toxic, Rainbow and Celestial mutations, fill your Index, hatch eggs for 15 pets that follow you and boost your garden, and join the events every 10 minutes: climb the Giant Beanstalk for a Magic Bean, Growth Frenzy, Golden Rain and Rainbow Bloom. Daily quests, daily rewards, gifts, the Beanstalk Wheel and codes, and no in-app purchases. Plants keep growing while you're away, so come back to a full garden!

Hosting steps

  1. GitHub: create the empty public repo asivixlp44-cmyk/build-a-garden-tower (no README), then the code is pushed to main.
  2. hosting.bloxity.io: create the app build-a-garden-tower and copy its deploy token.
  3. The token goes into the repo secret LEGION_DEPLOY_TOKEN; the workflow is re-run (it builds the server image and the client and deploys both).
  4. After the first deploy, make the GHCR package build-a-garden-tower-server public (github.com/asivixlp44-cmyk → Packages → Package settings → Change visibility).
  5. Check https://build-a-garden-tower.host.bloxity.io/health and play at https://build-a-garden-tower.play.bloxity.io.
  6. Create the game in Manage Games with the values above (slug exactly build-a-garden-tower).
  7. Saves: Legion provides MONGODB_URI, so /health should say "db": true.
